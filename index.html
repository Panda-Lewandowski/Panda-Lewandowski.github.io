I have been catching cybercriminals my whole life. Today, I am a Product Owner and Cybercrime Investigator at Group-IB, working at the intersection of product development and cybercrime investigation. My experience spans digital forensics, threat intelligence, and OSINT-driven investigations around the world. Moving between these areas has allowed me to develop a T-shaped skill set and turn practical investigation experience into products, methodologies, and training.

I do not believe investigations can be taught as a list of tools. The real craft is learning to form hypotheses, notice weak signals, and keep going when the first path fails. I turn that craft into products, methodologies, talks, and practical learning systems.

Quantified Self

I am part of the Quantified Self community. H. James Harrington said:
Measurement is the first step that leads to control and eventually to improvement. If you can’t measure something, you can’t understand it. If you can’t understand it, you can’t control it. If you can’t control it, you can’t improve it.
That is why I track the following types of data:
  • Sleep
  • Food
  • Mindfulness time
  • Body shape
  • Training
  • Blood
  • Thoughts
  • Finances
  • Supplements
  • Brain activity
